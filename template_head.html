Did changing up your game actually help?

Each point is one player: X = how much their mix changed between first and last season (change index), Y = how much their Impact percentile moved over that same span. If bigger changes tended to come with bigger percentile gains, expect the points to trend upward (positive r); if changing up their game didn't pay off, expect no trend (r near 0) or a downward one.

Players ranked by change index

Change index = how much a player's mix shifted between their first and last season with data (0 = no change, 100 = completely different mix; see the Change Index note on the Player Analysis tab). For bowling this is the average of two separate change indices — where they bowl (line/length grid) and how fast (pace category) — shown broken out in their own columns, since a bowler can change one without the other. "Change-index percentile" ranks the combined shift among every player with a valid change index for this metric, so you can see who changed the most relative to everyone else. "Δ percentile (benefit)" compares each player's own Impact percentile in their last season to their first, to show whether the change in approach coincided with better or worse performance.
