# Season mix-change summary

How much each player's / team's mix of deliveries changed between their first and last season in the data, and whether the Impact they got from each category changed with it.

* **Change index** -- half the sum of the absolute percentage-point changes in category share (0 = identical mix, 100 = completely different mix).
* **Biggest share mover** -- the category whose share of deliveries moved the most (`first% → last%`, change in pp).
* **Biggest Impact swing** -- the category whose Impact vs the role mean per ball moved the most (`first → last`, change ± combined standard error), among categories with at least 4 deliveries in both seasons (smaller samples have no reliable standard error).
* **Shifts > 2 SE** -- number of categories whose Impact change exceeds twice its combined standard error.
* Rankings only include entities with at least 60 deliveries in each of the two seasons.
